InThisWorld Studios

For Second Life and OpenSim grids such as OSgrid and Kitely.

One studio for everything we make for Second Life and OpenSim. Each part is named after the Marketplace category it sells in. This site shows what each studio does; the studio itself runs in our workshop.

StructuresWorking

Buildings, towers and whole city blocks, designed in the browser and built in-world prim by prim.

Floor PlansWorking

Lay out rooms on a plan, then raise the plan into a building.

FurnishingsWorking

Furniture, plants, lighting and artwork for homes and stores.

VehiclesIn development

Morphing vehicles and the shop tools to build and test them.

AvatarsWorking

AccessoriesWorking

Animated ObjectsWorking

Animesh creatures and characters, shaped in the Body Creator.

AnimationsPlanned

TerrainWorking

ProjectsWorking
