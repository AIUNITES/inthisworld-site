🛠️ Car Lift

v1.8

A two-post shop lift that holds a car up so you can see underneath it — and raises or lowers the car with the arms.

  • Fit — the lift finds the car above it, sets itself on the floor underneath, turns to match and swings its pads under the floor pan.
  • Moves the car — up and down in smooth steps, from the floor (on its wheels) to walk-under height.
  • Sync — one lift's buttons move every lift, so two cars go up together.
  • Click-through — only the yellow tab takes clicks; the rest lets you click the car behind it.

🎥 CamHUD3

v3.10

A fine camera HUD for builders and photographers: orbit and move in 1° / 1 cm steps, lock onto any object and look at it from every side.

  • Target lock — aim at a car and the camera stays on it, even while it moves.
  • Top and Bottom — straight down on the roof, or straight up at the underside; the under view stops just above the floor so the floor never hides the car.
  • Keys mode — arrows orbit, Page Up / Down zoom, shift slides.

📋 Vehicle Menu HUD

v3.5

One small worn button for every vehicle and gadget you own in the region.

  • Hold 1 second — a pick list of your cars, lifts and studio; open just the one you want, or All.
  • Tap — the car you are driving, or the menu you picked last.
  • Dash — shows the car's mode, speed and height while you drive.
[screenshot: the Car Studio from outside, see-through wall, grid look]

🎬 Car Studio

Stage v1.3

A photo and test stage for vehicles, with its own light rig.

  • Size — 20, 25 or 30 m. Shape — round or square.
  • Look — white, studio gradient, or a 1 m grid for judging size in photos.
  • See in, not out — from outside you look straight in; inside it is a full backdrop.
  • Lights — layouts, colours, effects and an LED sign.
[screenshot: riding the Work Skiff to a spot beside a car]

🚤 MoveTo

v1.4

Gives any vehicle a one-jump move with you aboard — saved places, teleport pads, or an exact spot. Our Work Skiff uses it to park you right beside the job, and the Car Lift uses it to carry cars up and down.

[screenshot: the Wedge Runner in car, boat and plane shapes]

🚗 Wedge Runner

in development

Our morphing prim vehicle: car, boat, foil, sub, plane and space jet in one, in two sizes (L and S). Menu Sync lets one menu drive two test cars at once.
