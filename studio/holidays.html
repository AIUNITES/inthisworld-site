Holiday DecorationsWorking

For Second Life and OpenSim grids such as OSgrid and Kitely.

Our Ulrichs home decorates itself. Each holiday is its own set in the house's rezzer box. A date script in the box checks the date (SLT) every hour, rezzes a set one to two weeks before its holiday (Christmas from Thanksgiving Day), and clears it the day after.

  • Dates are worked out each year, so Easter, Memorial Day, Labor Day and Thanksgiving move with the calendar
  • Only the sets that are due take up prim space
  • Turn any set on or off by hand from the box menu

How it works

  1. Each holiday's pieces are built as their own small objects and placed around the house: the tree by the stair hall, wreaths on the doors, light strings under the roof edge.
  2. Their positions go into the rezzer box under the holiday's name.
  3. The date script in the box works out this year's dates and rezzes or clears each set.
  4. When the house is closed for the night the sets clear with the floors, and come back when someone arrives.
